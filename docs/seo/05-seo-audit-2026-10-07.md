## New findings

### Critical

None.

### High

**H1. The 4 comparison pages are not in Google's index, and 3 have almost no internal links.**
- Evidence: URL Inspection 2026-10-07 — `/best-planning-poker-tools`, `/planning-poker-vs-estimation-meetings`, `/scrum-poker-online-without-ads` "URL is unknown to Google"; `/planningpokeronline-alternative` "Discovered – currently not indexed". In the repo, the last 3 are linked only from `client/index.html` and `client/public/best-planning-poker-tools/index.html` (plus `/llms.txt`). `/best-planning-poker-tools` is linked from 8 pages.
- Why it matters: these pages target the listicle and "alternative" intent that the baseline SERP check found. They earn nothing until Google indexes them. A low-authority host gets few crawls, so internal links decide what Google fetches first.
- Fix: (a) owner: URL Inspection → Request indexing for the 4 URLs; (b) add in-body links with descriptive anchors: Jira guide → `/planningpokeronline-alternative`; `/what-is-planning-poker` → `/planning-poker-vs-estimation-meetings`; remote-teams guide → `/scrum-poker-online-without-ads`; cross-link the 4 pages; add a "Related guides" block to the 4 EN guides.
- Done when: each of the 3 pages has ≥ 4 internal links from EN guides; URL Inspection shows all 4 crawled, and indexed or with a recorded reason, by 2026-10-21.

### Medium

- **M1.** No visible byline or date on guides. Author and dates exist only in JSON-LD. Add "By Alexander Bondarchuk · Updated YYYY-MM-DD" under each H1, and a short author block.
- **M2.** Mobile tap targets under 44 px on `/`: Create room 37 px high, cookie Accept/Decline 32 px, language select 27 px. The Create room button looks disabled (grey, no accent colour).
- **M3.** Sitemap `lastmod` follows build commits. 40 of 42 URLs say 2026-10-07 after a JSON-LD-only release. Google discounts `lastmod` that moves without content change. Bump it only for visible content changes.

### Low

- **L1.** `/glossary` (and translations) has no Article block and no `dateModified`; no `DefinedTermSet`.
- **L2.** Translated descriptions run 165–205 chars (de, es, fr, pt, ru, sr); fr/ru/es titles reach 74–78 chars. EN `/what-is-planning-poker` description is 160 chars.
- **L3.** Home H1 ("Free Online Planning Poker for Agile Teams") sits below the fold (y≈820–885 px); the large visible "Planning Poker" title is not a heading. Low impact for ranking.
- **L4.** Guides have no images or diagrams; the Jira guide is the exception. A Fibonacci or reveal visual helps AI answers and users.
- **L5.** Organization on `/` has no `logo` and no `@id`; no WebSite block.
- **L6.** Jira page has unsourced claims ("most of them charge per user"). Cite one source or soften.
- **L7.** CSP is report-only except `frame-ancestors`; HSTS has no `includeSubDomains`. Security hygiene, not ranking.
- **L8.** Competitor facts are dated 2026-10-05. Re-check by 2027-04 (roadmap says every 6 months).

**What works (keep):** everything in the baseline list, plus: one H1 per page; Person author; FAQPage = visible text; `/llms.txt`; all 42 sitemap URLs 200 with self canonicals and reciprocal hreflang; English-only pages carry only `en` + `x-default`; real 404 with `noindex`; AI search bots (OAI-SearchBot, ChatGPT-User, PerplexityBot, Claude-SearchBot) get 200. Cloudflare 403 for AI training bots is the owner's choice.
