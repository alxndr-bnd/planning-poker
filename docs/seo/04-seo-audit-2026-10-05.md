## Drop analysis: is −45% real?

**Digest numbers.** 52 vs 94 matches rolling 7-day windows (Sep 27–Oct 3: 51; Sep 20–26: 86). Calendar weeks give 55 vs 79 (−30%).

**Statistically real.** A Poisson count of 86 has σ ≈ 9; 51 is ~4σ lower. More decisive: seven weekly declines in a row, and the 28-day window is −59%.

**But it is not a ranking loss:**
1. Average position is flat (59–66 every week since 08-10). Per-query positions are flat too ("planning poker" 68 → 68).
2. Clicks were 0 before and after. Nothing a user felt changed.
3. The August level was a spike, not a baseline. Impressions went 47 → 222 → 356 in the two weeks after Google re-crawled most pages on 2026-08-10…13. They then decayed back toward the July level (~20–80/week).
4. 70–90% of impressions sit at position 61+ (page 7). Weekends get as many impressions as weekdays (Sat 207, Sun 193 vs Mon 218, Wed 252 over 8 weeks). Human B2B searches dip on weekends. So a large part of the deep-page impressions is likely rank trackers or scrapers, not scrum masters. This is an inference; GSC cannot prove it.

**What did lose ground, and matters:**
- **Home page**: 236 → 42 impressions. Query breadth fell from 36 to 8–14 queries/week. "planning poker online" (pos 24–39) went 9 → 4 → 2 → 1 → 0 → 0 → 0 per week from 08-17. These are the story's target queries.
- No site change lines up with the start of the decline (week of 08-31). Releases: v0.41.0 (08-20, analytics tags), v0.42–43 (09-04, OG images); SEO-relevant changes came only from 09-26. Google ran a spam update from 2026-08-18 (source: coalitiontechnologies.com). The timing fits SERP churn after it, but this is not provable.
- Two long-standing home-page weaknesses probably made the home page easy to drop: (a) until 2026-10-01 React replaced the prerendered landing in `#root`, so the **rendered** DOM had no H1, no FAQ, no landing text (fixed by commit `7df8242`, SERBITO-350); (b) field CLS was 1.0 on mobile (same commit). Google last crawled `/` on 2026-09-26 and has not seen the fix.

**Recommendation for the digest:** at this volume, raw impressions are noise-dominated. Track clicks, impressions at position ≤ 30, and the count of indexed URLs instead.
