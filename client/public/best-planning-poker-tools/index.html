Short answer: which tool fits which team

  • You want no ads, no account and open source: Serbito Planning Poker. It is free, MIT-licensed, and you can run your own copy.
  • You need T-shirt sizes or your own card values: scrumpoker-online.org (custom values with a free account) or planningpokeronline.com (Fibonacci, T-shirt sizes or custom decks). Our tool has the Fibonacci deck only.
  • You want to estimate inside Jira: planningpokeronline.com has a Jira plugin on the Atlassian Marketplace. Our tool and scrumpoker-online.org have no Jira sync: you type the points into Jira yourself.
  • You also run retrospectives: pointingpoker.com has retrospective sessions next to its pointing sessions.

Serbito Planning Poker

A free, open-source planning poker in the browser. You type a name, create a room and share the link. Votes stay hidden until the person with the star reveals them. The reveal shows each name with a card, a count per card and a consensus mark when all numbers match. You can type an issue key or title for each round, and the estimate log lists the revealed rounds with their titles. Observers can follow without a card. The interface has 9 languages.

What it does not do: no custom decks, no timer, no average, no export, no Jira or CSV import, and rooms are not saved. Custom decks, export, issue import and saved rooms are open for votes on GitHub: we build what gets votes. The site counts visits with Google Analytics after you accept the cookie banner, and with Cloudflare Web Analytics. The privacy page lists what they get.

planningpokeronline.com

A planning poker by We Agile You with a free Basic plan and a paid Premium plan. The free plan allows 9 votings per game and 5 issues voted per game, with unlimited games for 6 weeks. Premium costs $30 per facilitator per month or $300 billed yearly, with a 15-day trial. It removes those limits and adds saved games, history and more facilitators. The site lists a Linear integration, CSV import, pasting from the clipboard and a Jira plugin on the Atlassian Marketplace. Choose it when you need those integrations and a paid plan is fine. More detail: a free alternative to planningpokeronline.com.

scrumpoker-online.org

A free tool with no sign-up: you open a room and share the room ID. Free rooms show ads. Premium costs 40 USD a year and gives an ad-free room for everyone who joins, a voting timer, automatic average and median, a personal room ID and an attendance indicator. A free account lets you set custom card values, for example T-shirt sizes. It has no native Jira sync. Choose it when you need custom decks or a timer, and ads or a small fee are fine. See also scrum poker online without ads.

pointingpoker.com

A free tool with no downloads and no sign-up. It says that it is "sustained by unobtrusive ads" and that it collects no personal data. Next to pointing sessions, it offers retrospective sessions. Choose it when you want a simple free tool and ads are fine for your team.

How to choose

  1. Check the limits against your session. Count the items you size in one meeting. A cap of 5 issues per game can be too small for one refinement session.
  2. Check what your screen shows. If you share your screen with a client, the ads in the room go with it.
  3. Check who needs an account. Guests and contractors join faster with a link and a name.
  4. Check the deck. If your team estimates in T-shirt sizes, pick a tool with that deck.
  5. Check where the data goes. Read the privacy page. If the data must stay in house, pick an open-source tool that you can host yourself.
