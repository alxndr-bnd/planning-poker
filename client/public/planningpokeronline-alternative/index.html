As of October 2026. Facts about other tools were checked on 2026-10-05 on each vendor's own website.

planningpokeronline.com is a capable tool, and its free plan has limits. If your team hits them, Serbito Planning Poker is a free alternative with no cap on rounds and no paid plan. This page compares the two. Facts about planningpokeronline.com come from its pricing page; facts about our tool come from its code. We also say where their tool is the better choice.

What the free plan of planningpokeronline.com includes

  • 9 votings per game and 5 issues voted per game.
  • Unlimited games for 6 weeks.
  • No limit on players, permanent game URLs and CSV import.

Premium costs $30 per facilitator per month or $300 billed yearly, with a 15-day trial. It removes the voting and issue limits, keeps your created games and their history, lets you resume a game at any time, and lets you add or remove facilitators.

When planningpokeronline.com is the better choice

  • You want to estimate inside Jira with a plugin, or you work in Linear.
  • You import a backlog from CSV and vote through it item by item.
  • Your team uses T-shirt sizes or a custom deck.
  • You want to resume a game later or keep its history (Premium).
  • Several facilitators share one account (Premium).

When Serbito Planning Poker fits better

  • Long refinement sessions. There is no cap on votings, so the tenth story costs nothing extra.
  • Guests and contractors. People type a name and join. Nobody makes an account.
  • No budget. There is no paid plan and no trial that ends.
  • Data that must stay in house. The code is MIT-licensed, and you can run your own copy in one container.
  • Teams in several languages. The interface has 9 languages and opens in the browser's language.

Switch in two minutes

  1. Open the free planning poker app, type your name and click Create room. The invite link goes to your clipboard.
  2. Paste the link into the call chat. Teammates open it, type a name and join.
  3. Type the issue key or title in the Item title field and press Enter. Everybody votes, the person with the star reveals, and the team discusses the highest and the lowest card.
  4. Click New vote to vote again, or go to the next issue. Type the agreed number into your tracker: the room sends nothing to Jira or Linear.

Questions

Is Serbito Planning Poker really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan, no trial and no ads. The code is open source under the MIT license.

Can I import issues from CSV or Jira?

Not today. Issue import is a feature vote on GitHub. Add your vote if you need it.

Are rooms and results saved?

No. Rooms live in server memory. The estimate log shows the revealed rounds while the room is open, and the room is deleted soon after everyone leaves.
